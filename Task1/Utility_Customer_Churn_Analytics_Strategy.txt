My Senior Data Scientist and I discussed the business problem regarding customer churn in a gas and electricity utility company. Based on our understanding, key factors influencing whether a customer stays with or switches an energy provider may include Payment history, pricing, customer service quality, purchasing behavior, and business location.

To validate these assumptions, we would need detailed customer data from the client. Once the data is available, our approach would include:

Conducting exploratory data analysis (EDA) to understand patterns and trends

Performing data cleaning and preprocessing

Implementing feature engineering for both categorical and numerical variables

Building and evaluating multiple machine learning models to identify the best-performing solution

Creating interactive dashboards using a data visualization tool to clearly communicate insights and support data-driven decision-making

This structured approach will help identify the key drivers of churn and provide actionable insights to the client.
